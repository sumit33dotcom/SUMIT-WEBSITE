📐 Mathematics — Chapters

1. Real Numbers ＋

📝 Notes

Euclid's division algorithm is used to find HCF. Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic states that every composite number has a unique prime factorisation.

📌 Formula

a = bq + r
0 ≤ r < b

HCF × LCM = Product of two positive integers

❓ Important Question

Find HCF of 135 and 225 using Euclid's algorithm.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Prove that √5 is irrational.
2. Polynomials ＋

📝 Notes

A polynomial can be linear, quadratic or cubic depending on its degree. Zeroes are values of x for which the polynomial becomes zero.

📌 Formula

For ax² + bx + c:
Sum of zeroes = −b/a
Product of zeroes = c/a

❓ Important Question

Find the zeroes of x² − 5x + 6.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes have sum 7 and product 10.
3. Pair of Linear Equations ＋

📝 Notes

Two linear equations in two variables may have one solution, no solution or infinitely many solutions.

📌 Formula

a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂ → Unique solution
a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ → No solution
a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂ → Infinite solutions

❓ Important Question

Solve: 2x + 3y = 13 and 3x − 2y = 4.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find the value of k for which a pair of equations has infinitely many solutions.
4. Quadratic Equations ＋

📝 Notes

A quadratic equation has the general form ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0.

📌 Formula

x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac))/2a

D = b² − 4ac

❓ Important Question

Solve x² − 7x + 12 = 0.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Determine the nature of roots using the discriminant.
5. Arithmetic Progressions ＋

📝 Notes

An AP is a sequence in which the difference between consecutive terms is constant.

📌 Formula

aₙ = a + (n−1)d
Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n−1)d]

❓ Important Question

Find the 20th term of 3, 7, 11, 15...

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find the sum of the first 25 terms of an AP.
6. Triangles ＋

📝 Notes

Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides.

📌 Formula

Corresponding sides of similar triangles are proportional.

❓ Important Question

State and prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Prove two given triangles are similar using AA criterion.
7. Coordinate Geometry ＋

📝 Notes

Coordinate geometry connects algebra with geometry using points represented by ordered pairs.

📌 Formula

Distance = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]

Midpoint = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)

❓ Important Question

Find the distance between (2,3) and (6,6).

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find the coordinates of the point dividing a line segment in a given ratio.
8. Introduction to Trigonometry ＋

📝 Notes

Trigonometric ratios relate the sides of a right triangle to its acute angles.

📌 Formula

sin θ = P/H
cos θ = B/H
tan θ = P/B

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

❓ Important Question

If sin θ = 3/5, find cos θ and tan θ.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Prove a trigonometric identity using basic ratios.
9. Applications of Trigonometry ＋

📝 Notes

Heights and distances problems use angles of elevation and depression with trigonometric ratios.

📌 Formula

tan θ = Height / Distance

❓ Important Question

Find the height of an object when its distance and angle of elevation are given.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Solve a tower-and-observer height problem using tan θ.
10. Circles ＋

📝 Notes

The tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

📌 Formula

Radius ⟂ Tangent at point of contact

❓ Important Question

Prove that tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find the tangent length when radius and distance from centre are known.
11. Areas Related to Circles ＋

📝 Notes

Questions involve circumference, area, sectors and segments of circles.

📌 Formula

Circumference = 2πr
Area = πr²
Sector Area = θ/360 × πr²

❓ Important Question

Find the area of a circle of radius 7 cm.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find the area of a sector for a given radius and central angle.
12. Surface Areas & Volumes ＋

📝 Notes

This chapter covers surface area and volume of combinations of solids such as cylinders, cones and spheres.

📌 Formula

Cylinder Volume = πr²h
Sphere Volume = 4/3πr³
Cone Volume = 1/3πr²h

❓ Important Question

Find the volume of a cylinder with given radius and height.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Solve a combination-of-solids volume problem.
13. Statistics ＋

📝 Notes

Statistics deals with collection, presentation and analysis of data. Important measures include mean, median and mode.

📌 Formula

Mean = Σfx / Σf

Median = l + [(n/2 − cf)/f] × h

Mode = l + [(f₁−f₀)/(2f₁−f₀−f₂)] × h

❓ Important Question

Find the mean of a grouped frequency distribution.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find the median of a grouped data table.
14. Probability ＋

📝 Notes

Probability measures how likely an event is to occur. Its value lies between 0 and 1.

📌 Formula

P(E) = Favourable outcomes / Total outcomes

P(E) + P(not E) = 1

❓ Important Question

Find the probability of getting an even number when a die is thrown.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Find the probability of an event from a given set of equally likely outcomes.

🔬 Science — Quick Revision

Chemical Reactions & Equations ＋

📝 Notes

Chemical reactions involve formation of new substances. Equations should be balanced according to conservation of mass.

📌 Formula / Key Point

Combination • Decomposition • Displacement • Double Displacement • Redox

❓ Question

Why should a chemical equation be balanced?

🔥 PYQ-Style

Identify the type of reaction in a given equation.
Acids, Bases & Salts ＋

📝 Notes

Acids generally produce H⁺ ions in aqueous solution while bases produce OH⁻ ions.

📌 Formula

pH < 7 → Acidic
pH = 7 → Neutral
pH > 7 → Basic

❓ Question

What does the pH scale indicate?

🔥 PYQ-Style

Arrange given solutions according to increasing pH.
Metals & Non-metals ＋

📝 Notes

Metals are generally malleable, ductile and good conductors. Non-metals generally show opposite properties.

📌 Key Point

Metal + Oxygen → Metal Oxide

❓ Question

Why are metals generally good conductors of electricity?

🔥 PYQ-Style

Explain the reactivity series with an example.
Carbon & Its Compounds ＋

📝 Notes

Carbon forms covalent bonds and shows catenation, allowing formation of many compounds.

📌 Key Point

Carbon valency = 4

❓ Question

What is catenation?

🔥 PYQ-Style

Differentiate saturated and unsaturated hydrocarbons.
Life Processes ＋

📝 Notes

Main life processes include nutrition, respiration, transportation and excretion.

📌 Key Point

Photosynthesis: CO₂ + H₂O → Glucose + O₂

❓ Question

Explain the role of stomata in plants.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Compare aerobic and anaerobic respiration.
Electricity ＋

📝 Notes

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.

📌 Formula

V = IR
P = VI
P = I²R
P = V²/R

❓ Question

Calculate current when voltage and resistance are given.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Solve a series/parallel resistance numerical.
Light — Reflection & Refraction ＋

📌 Formula

1/f = 1/v + 1/u
m = hᵢ/hₒ = v/u

❓ Question

State the laws of reflection.

🔥 PYQ-Style

Solve a mirror formula numerical.
Human Eye & Colourful World ＋

📝 Notes

The human eye forms an image on the retina. Common defects include myopia and hypermetropia.

❓ Question

What is myopia and how is it corrected?

🔥 PYQ-Style

Explain dispersion of white light.
